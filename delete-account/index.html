Delete your account

You can permanently delete your Bokheim account here without installing or opening the app.

This deletes your account, email address, password, synced library records, reading progress, bookmarks and saved feed settings from the account database. All sessions are revoked, and your account loses access to uploaded books and covers.

Books already saved on your devices stay on those devices. Uploaded book and cover files may remain on the server, but this account will no longer have access to them.

Account deletion cannot be undone.

Confirm which account to delete

Your password is checked when you confirm deletion. Forgot your password? Reset it in Bokheim.
